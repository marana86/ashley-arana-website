Effective Date: June 13, 2026 • ashleyaranahomes.com

California Residents: This Privacy Policy complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). See Section 7 for your specific rights.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website ashleyaranahomes.com, owned and operated by Ashley Arana, licensed California REALTOR® (CA DRE #02007681) with Main Street Realtors ("we," "us," or "our").

By using this website or submitting any form, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill out a contact, home valuation, or buyer/seller inquiry form
  • Call or text the phone number listed on this website
  • Email us directly
  • Sign up for any newsletter or market update list

The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address or location (for home valuations or listing inquiries)
  • General information about your real estate needs (buying timeline, price range, etc.)
  • IP address and browser/device information collected automatically through analytics tools

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your real estate inquiry or request
  • Provide you with a home valuation or market analysis
  • Follow up via phone, email, or text regarding your real estate needs
  • Send relevant real estate market updates, if you have opted in
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Main Street Realtors: Ashley's licensed brokerage, for transaction and compliance purposes
  • Technology providers: Email platforms, CRM, and analytics tools used to operate this website (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Legal requirements: Law enforcement or courts if required by law

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website uses cookies and tracking tools to understand how visitors use the site and to measure advertising effectiveness. We use:

  • Google Analytics — anonymized traffic and behavior data
  • Google Ads (gtag) — to measure form submission conversions from our advertising campaigns
  • Session cookies — expire when you close your browser

You may disable cookies through your browser settings without affecting your ability to use this website.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of any information you transmit to us.

7. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

As a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months
  • Delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions
  • Opt out of any marketing communications at any time
  • Non-discrimination — we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within 45 days.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of this website after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
