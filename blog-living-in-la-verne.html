Community Guide • La Verne

Living in La Verne, CA: What Makes It Great

By Ashley Arana • Main Street Realtors • July 2026

Tucked into the foothills of the San Gabriel Valley, La Verne is the kind of place people describe as "the town that feels like it stopped rushing." Here's a quick look at why so many residents never want to leave.

A Real Small-Town Feel

Old Town La Verne still has that classic charm — tree-lined streets, local shops, and neighbors who actually know each other. It's a rare thing to find this close to the sprawl of Los Angeles County.

Home to a Top University

The University of La Verne anchors the community with a steady stream of culture, events, and energy — without the traffic and noise of a big campus town.

Outdoor Access Without Leaving Home

Marshall Canyon and the surrounding foothill trails put hiking, biking, and open space just minutes from most neighborhoods — a huge draw for anyone who wants nature without a long drive.

Strong, Community-Focused Schools

Bonita Unified serves La Verne with a reputation families trust — one more reason this city keeps showing up on "best places to raise a family" lists across the Inland Empire.
