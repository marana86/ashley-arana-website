Community Guide • Glendora

Living in Glendora, CA: What Makes It Great

By Ashley Arana • Main Street Realtors • July 2026

Glendora is one of those cities that flies under the radar — a tight-knit community of about 50,000 people at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains, with a lot more going for it than most people realize. Here's a quick look.

A Village Worth Slowing Down For

Glendora Village is the walkable heart of the city — local coffee shops, restaurants, and community events that make weekends feel like an actual break, not just an errand run.

Trails and Parks at the Foothills

South Hills Park, Big Dalton Canyon Wilderness Area, and Finkbiner Park give residents genuinely great hiking and open space minutes from home — a big part of why outdoor-minded buyers love this city.

One of the Safest Cities Around

Glendora consistently ranks as one of the safest cities in Southern California, and was even named the "Most Playful" city in the San Gabriel Valley — a fun distinction that reflects the community's genuine family-friendly energy.

Solid Schools, Well-Connected Location

Glendora Unified serves more than 6,200 students across its elementary, middle, and high schools, and the 210 and 57 freeways put Pasadena, Los Angeles, and Orange County all within easy reach.
