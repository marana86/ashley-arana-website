Living in Upland, CA: What Makes It Great

By Ashley Arana • Main Street Realtors • July 2026

Upland has carried the nickname "City of Gracious Living" for decades — and once you spend a little time here, it's easy to understand why. Here's a quick look at what makes it special.

Euclid Avenue — A Street Like No Other

The famous tree-lined median running down Euclid Avenue gives Upland an identity all its own — historic, walkable, and instantly recognizable to anyone who's driven through.

Mountain Views, Everywhere

Sitting right at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains, Upland offers postcard-worthy views from a huge share of its neighborhoods — a daily reminder of why people love living here.

A Diverse, Well-Established Community

Upland has a genuine mix of long-time residents and newer families, giving it a settled, welcoming feel rather than the transient energy of some faster-growing cities nearby.

Convenience Without the Congestion

Ontario International Airport sits right next door, and central freeway access puts the rest of the Inland Empire and LA within easy reach — without Upland itself feeling crowded.
